P[7]: Programmes Use Cases
P[16]: Purpose
P[17]: The purpose of this document is to show the data required when an SDP/Employer/Non-Employer Entity/Learner/ merSETA Representative/HEI/TVET/Organisation captures and submits an application for employed and unemployed learners against the other learning programs.
P[18]: This data will be stored and processed in NSDMS 2.0 to enable compliance with:
P[19]: National Learners’ Record Database (NLRD) and the 
P[20]: Skills Education and Training Management Information System (SETMIS) to ensure compliance.
P[21]: Other business requirements
P[23]: The data requirements in this document will ensure that the quality, accuracy, and completeness of the data collected will enable the merSETA to manage its performance vis a vis the results that are required to be reported to the relevant bodies as per the Annual Performance Plan and other learner reporting requirements. The data will be made accessible to all authorised users.
P[25]: In Scope
P[26]: This document will aim to show the following:
P[27]: The data associated with a learner application include the following critical data entities:
P[28]: Primary Data
P[29]: Learner
P[30]: Referenced Data
P[31]: Provider
P[32]: Employer / non employer entity
P[33]: Organising Framework for Occupations (OFO)
P[34]: NQF
P[35]: Learning programs
P[37]: Notes: 
P[38]: This use case references the Learner Management – Learner registration for all other learning programs
P[39]: All non-functional requirements will be specified in the full Functional Requirements Specification.

R13: Rejection | The act of not accepting the application to register a learner as the requirements for registration based on verification of submitted learner data and records for compliance, accuracy and completeness has not been met.
R14: Role | A defined action within a workflow/process to be performed by a team member

R16: Skills Development Providers | An accredited learning institution that delivers occupationally based learning
R17: Submission | The action of presenting a document to the merSETA for consideration in any training learning program including but not limited to learner registrations, continuations, completions, assessments, moderation, and trade testing either in an electronic format or hard copy
R18: Structured Data | Structured data is data that adheres to a pre-defined data model and is therefore straightforward to analyse. Structured data conforms to a tabular format with relationship between the different rows and columns.
R19: Unemployed learner | A learner that is employed for the purpose of the learning programme.
R20: Unstructured Data | Unstructured data is information that either does not have a predefined data model or is not organised in a pre-defined manner. Unstructured information is typically text-heavy, but may contain data such as dates, numbers, and facts as well.
R21: Withdrawn | The action of withdrawing an application to register a learner before merSETA registering a learner.
R22: Work-Based Learning Programme Agreement | An agreement that is entered into by the respective parties for the delivery of a learning programmes.
